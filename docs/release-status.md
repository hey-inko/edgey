## Owner decisions before opening up (PRD §8)

- **Catalogue:** Approve supplier fit and evidence for each published variant and alias, confirm its physical pick label and enter actual opening stock. Leave unverified variants in draft.
- **Commercial and Finance:** Approve the price matrix, all-units discounts and margin after delivery, VAT status and invoice details, customer terms and returns. Check both a card PDF and a Net 30 PDF in Stripe's sandbox against the saved amounts. Card passes VAT-inclusive amounts with a text breakdown; Net 30 passes explicit line tax amounts. Neither a matching total nor a test invoice establishes statutory compliance.
- **Fulfilment:** Confirm the carrier, cut-off, coverage and services by postcode, holiday calendar, packaging and sample eligibility. Have a qualified adviser or Trading Standards confirm any remote-sale, age and delivery checks for the actual products and destinations before offering them.
- **Operations:** Name the people who pick, answer enquiries, approve samples and handle failed or returned orders. Supply the official business name, approved email and address for the contact page. Publish the full privacy notice, including who controls the data, purpose, sharing, retention and how to exercise rights. Set the approved notice URL, agree the retention and redaction rules for intake, Finder events, approved trials and applications, orders and unconfirmed Auth accounts, then schedule and monitor the purge before accepting public submissions.
- **Finance (credit):** Approve initial limits, approval and suspension rules, ownership of overdue invoices and how to reconcile them. Run the full 30-day invoice lifecycle in staging before enabling Net 30.

Use the [design acceptance pass](../design.md#visual-acceptance-pass) to review extension screens in both themes before the first live release.

## Acceptance evidence

- `pnpm test:acceptance` covers the card, trial and Net 30 acceptance scenarios in PRD §7 and the §1 weekend journey. Net 30 runs the concurrent checkout and timeout cases against local Supabase, then drives finance approval, checkout, dispatch, partial payment and settlement through the production build in Chromium. All provider responses are simulated; webhook verification uses signed request bytes. The visual checklist uses separate Chromium captures.
- The staff requests desk shows enquiry reply and trial message state. “Sent” means the provider accepted the message, not that it arrived. The contact and privacy pages remain informational until approved content is supplied. Keep `EDGEY_INDEXING_ENABLED=false` and `EDGEY_FINDER_ATTRIBUTION_ENABLED=false` in staging; hosted attribution also requires an approved notice and buyer opt-in.
- The commercial report uses retained data, not a complete customer cohort. An exact search with no attributed checkout or a dispatched trial with no confirmed purchase link is not proof there was no purchase. Exact-match conversion covers only qualifying links from consenting browsers; the report still counts all exact searches.
- Run the [separate Finder measurements](staging.md#6-run-the-weekend-staging-checklist-on-the-deployed-site) in hosted staging. The audit baseline passed the 50 ms warm-RPC target locally; the mobile-emulated figure is a separate browser metric, not provider or device evidence.
